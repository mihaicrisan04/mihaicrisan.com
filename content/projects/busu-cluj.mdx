busu shows every bus, tram and trolleybus in cluj-napoca, live on the map. it started as a rewrite of the bus tracker i built while commuting to UBB, and turned into a proper product: a native iOS app, a native Android app, a landing page and a backend i host myself. 1.0 is out on the App Store, free, no account, no ads.

## the live map

every vehicle in the city is a line badge on the map, moving every 20 seconds. tap one and you get its route, the direction it's going, a ticket and the timetable in one sheet. the opposite direction fades out so the one you care about stays readable.

## arrivals and schedules

tap a stop and you see what's coming, in minutes and stops away. the schedules tab keeps your lines on top with a big countdown to the next departure, and the full timetable splits weekdays, saturday and sunday with the next bus highlighted.

## every stop on the route

each line has its full stop rail, with the lines you can connect to at every stop. buying a ticket is right there too: the SMS ticket or the 24pay app opens straight from the line sheet, prefilled for that line.

<ProjectImage src="https://ik.imagekit.io/mihaicrisan/projects/busu-cluj/gallery/05-stops.jpg" alt="line 25's stops with connecting lines at each stop, next to the line sheet with SMS and 24pay ticket buttons" caption="stops, connections, and a ticket in two taps" />

## vinerea verde

on fridays, public transport in cluj is free. busu can remind you friday morning to leave the car at home. with location access it learns where home is, on the phone, and nudges you the moment you head out; without it, the reminder comes at 7:30. nothing leaves the device.

<ProjectImage src="https://ik.imagekit.io/mihaicrisan/projects/busu-cluj/gallery/06-vinerea-verde.jpg" alt="the Vinerea Verde settings page and the sheet explaining the leave-home reminder" caption="free rides every friday, and a reminder that knows when you leave home" />

## the data problem

the feed is big and fast: ~170 lines, ~915 stops, ~118k shape points and 400+ vehicles moving every 20 seconds. the app splits it into three tiers so the phone only does work it has to:

| data | lives in | refreshed |
|---|---|---|
| live vehicles | memory, via a Convex subscription | every 20 seconds |
| routes | SwiftData | at most every 2 minutes |
| shapes and stops | disk, lazy per route | kept forever |

the backend is just as lazy, because it has to be: Tranzy gives me **5000 requests a day**, and vehicles at a 20 second cadence alone would eat most of it. so the realtime crons are **heartbeat-gated**. the apps ping the backend about once a minute while they're open, the first ping arms the crons, and they disarm themselves five minutes after the last one. nobody looking, no polling.

## two native apps

no cross-platform layer. the iOS app is SwiftUI with a hand-written `MKMapView` wrapper doing its own clustering and differential annotation updates, because the stock SwiftUI map couldn't keep up with hundreds of moving badges. the Android app is Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on Mapbox, rebuilt to match iOS feature for feature, with the same analytics events behind the same single opt-out.

<ProjectImage src="https://ik.imagekit.io/mihaicrisan/projects/busu-cluj/gallery/07-android.jpg" alt="the schedules screen on iOS next to the same screen on Android" caption="same app, built twice: SwiftUI on the left, Jetpack Compose on the right" />

## purple, everywhere

purple is cluj's colour, so it's busu's. every line renders in a shade of it and the shade is the mode: trams darkest, buses lightest, flipped in dark mode so it stays readable. the type is rounded and bold, the icon is two rows of it, and one design doc keeps iOS, Android and the web in sync.

<ProjectImage src="https://ik.imagekit.io/mihaicrisan/projects/busu-cluj/gallery/08-icon.jpg" alt="the bu/su app icon in its light and dark variants over a purple transit-line pattern" caption="bu / su, light and dark" />

## stack notes

- **monorepo**: bun + turborepo with the iOS app, the Android app, the Convex backend, the Next.js landing page and a screenshot composer
- **backend**: Convex, self-hosted on my Hetzner VPS through Dokploy since september, with separate dev and prod instances
- **Xcode-free**: the `.xcodeproj` is gitignored and generated from `project.yml` with XcodeGen, built and run from the terminal
- **store screenshots are code**: a small Playwright app frames raw simulator captures, sets the headlines in romanian and english and renders every size App Store Connect asks for
- **privacy**: no account, location stays on the phone, PostHog and Sentry only with consent

## status

1.0 is live on the App Store in romanian and english. the Android app is release-ready and Google Play is next, and v2 is already planned.
